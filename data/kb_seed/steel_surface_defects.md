# Steel surface defect root causes

Defect: crazing. Material: hot-rolled steel. Process: hot rolling.
Cause: thermal fatigue of the roll surface or excessive cooling rate producing a network of fine surface cracks. Effect: reduced fatigue life and paint adhesion. Precautions: control cooling water flow, regrind rolls on schedule, monitor strip surface temperature.

Defect: inclusion. Material: hot-rolled steel. Process: steelmaking and casting.
Cause: non-metallic particles such as oxides or slag trapped during casting and carried into the strip. Effect: local weak points, cracking during forming. Precautions: improve ladle skimming and tundish filtration, calcium treatment, check slab cleanliness before rolling.

Defect: patches. Material: hot-rolled steel. Process: hot rolling.
Cause: uneven scale removal or localized roll contamination leaving discoloured patches on the surface. Effect: cosmetic rejection and inconsistent coating. Precautions: maintain descaling nozzle pressure, clean work rolls, verify pickling bath concentration.

Defect: pitted_surface. Material: hot-rolled steel. Process: pickling and rolling.
Cause: rolled-in scale falling off or acid over-pickling leaving small cavities. Effect: reduced fatigue strength and corrosion initiation sites. Precautions: control pickling time and inhibitor level, improve descaling before finishing stands.

Defect: rolled-in_scale. Material: hot-rolled steel. Process: hot rolling.
Cause: iron oxide scale pressed into the surface when descaling is insufficient. Effect: rough scaly texture and coating defects. Precautions: raise descaling water pressure, shorten time between descaling and rolling, inspect nozzles for blockage.

Defect: scratches. Material: hot-rolled steel. Process: handling and finishing.
Cause: contact with hard particles or sharp guide edges and rollers during transport and coiling. Effect: stress concentration lines and visual rejection. Precautions: polish guides and rollers, add protective liners, review coil handling and conveyor alignment.

Defect: dent. Process: forming and handling.
Cause: foreign particles on tools or impact during handling. Effect: local thickness loss and fit-up problems. Precautions: clean dies before each batch, use padded racks, add handling training.
